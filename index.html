Welcome to Kenya Snake Guide

Explore the fascinating world of snakes found in Kenya. Learn about their characteristics, habitats and role in the environment.

This educational website promotes wildlife awareness and safe behaviour around snakes.

Meet Some of Kenya's Snakes

Black Mamba

Scientific name: Dendroaspis polylepis

A fast-moving venomous snake found in parts of Kenya.

Puff Adder

Scientific name: Bitis arietans

A heavy-bodied venomous snake known for its camouflage.

Kenyan Sand Boa

Scientific name: Eryx colubrinus

A non-venomous boa that spends much of its time in sand.

Snake Safety

  • Keep a safe distance from every snake.
  • Never try to catch, handle or kill a snake.
  • If someone is bitten, seek emergency medical care immediately.
  • Do not cut the wound or attempt to suck out venom.

Remember: Never identify a snake by approaching it.

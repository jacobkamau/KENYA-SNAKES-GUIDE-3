Snake Gallery

Black Mamba

Highly venomous

A fast-moving African snake found in parts of Kenya. It usually prefers to escape when given space.

Puff Adder

Highly venomous

A thick-bodied snake with distinctive patterns. Its camouflage can make it difficult to notice.

Egyptian Cobra

Highly venomous

A large cobra found in parts of Africa. Never approach a cobra or attempt to handle one.

African Rock Python

Non-venomous

A very large constrictor. Although non-venomous, it can still cause serious injury.

Boomslang

Highly venomous

A slender, tree-dwelling snake. Its appearance varies, and bites require urgent medical care.

Eastern Green Mamba

Highly venomous

A green, mainly tree-dwelling snake associated with coastal forests in East Africa.

⚠️ Snake Safety Guide

  • Keep your distance from every unidentified snake.
  • Never attempt to catch, kill or handle a snake.
  • Wear closed shoes when walking in areas with vegetation.
  • If bitten, seek emergency medical care immediately.
  • Keep the bitten person still and the affected limb immobilised. Do not cut, suck or tightly tie the wound.
  • Do not wait for symptoms or attempt to catch the snake for identification.
